Total: **279 spec files** originally, **3828+ test cases** (lower bound; 180 .each blocks expand to more), plus v3.9.x additions noted inline.

Rule: a spec is ported BEFORE its implementation (red first), every `test()` case, no sampling.
Status: `pending` until the ported Solid spec passes with assertions unchanged (Tier A) / golden-anchored (Tier B).
Status `skip`: intentionally not ported — a React-ism with a framework-idiomatic Solid replacement (noted in the row).
Status `tier-b`: the verbatim recharts spec is coupled to React render semantics (e.g. render counts); its behavior is covered by an equivalent Solid spec instead.
Status `partial`: a subset of the spec's cases is ported and green; the remainder is deferred until the features they exercise land (noted in the row).
Port timing follows PORT_ORDER.md — a spec comes online when its source unit/slice does.
